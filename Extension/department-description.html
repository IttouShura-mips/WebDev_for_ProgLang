College of Computer Studies

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

4-Year Program
BSCS

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) is a comprehensive 4-year degree program focused on the study of computing concepts, algorithmic problem solving, advanced software engineering paradigms, intelligent systems, and computational theory. The curriculum is meticulously designed to foster technical proficiency, creative coding innovation, and deep analytical reasoning to build robust applications for contemporary industrial sectors.

Program Objectives

  • Design, test, and implement complex, scalable software solutions across multiple tech platforms.
  • Apply high-level theoretical and mathematical principles of computing to solve intricate real-world automation problems.
  • Develop secure, optimal, and ethical computational applications that adhere strictly to international quality standards.
  • Cultivate teamwork, project management capabilities, and agile methodology adaptation for enterprise tech environments.

Curriculum Timeline

  • Year 1: Programming Fundamentals, Discrete Mathematics, Intro to Computing, Data Structures
  • Year 2: Object-Oriented Programming, Computer Architecture, Relational Databases, Web Design
  • Year 3: Advanced Software Engineering, Algorithms, Operating Systems, Server-Side Web Dev, HCI
  • Year 4: AI & Machine Learning, Capstone Research Project, Industry Internship

Career Opportunities

  • Software Engineer / Developer, Systems Analyst, Full Stack Web Developer, Data Scientist, AI Specialist, Database Administrator, IT Solutions Architect
Requirements: Form 138, Good Moral Certificate, PSA Birth Certificate, Entrance Assessment Result

College of Arts & Governance

Bachelor of Science in Public Administration

4-Year Program
BSPA

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Public Administration (BSPA) program is structured to prepare students for effective executive and leadership roles within government agencies, policy formulation bureaus, non-profit institutions, and civic organizations. It emphasizes organizational theory, transparent public service stewardship, fiscal administration, and community development management.

Program Objectives

  • Master public policy formulation, evaluation frameworks, and community management procedures.
  • Cultivate accountable, transparent, highly responsive, and service-oriented administrative leadership.
  • Execute efficient bureaucratic and organizational strategies across various levels of government sectors.
  • Promote civic engagement and sustainable socio-economic governance programs.

Curriculum Timeline

  • Year 1: Intro to Public Administration, Philippine Governance & Politics, Administrative Ethics
  • Year 2: Organization & Management Theory, Local Legislation, Public Personnel Management
  • Year 3: Public Fiscal Administration, Public Policy Analysis, Government Budgeting, Public Finance
  • Year 4: Research Methods in Public Admin, Comprehensive Public Sector Internship

Career Opportunities

  • Public Policy Analyst, Civil Servant / Government Officer, HR Specialist, City / Municipal Administrator, NGO Executive Director, Legislative Staff
Requirements: Form 138, Good Moral Certificate, PSA Birth Certificate, Admission Screening Interview

College of Hospitality & Tourism

Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management

4-Year Program
BSTM

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management (BSTM) equips professionals with deep operational skills and administrative know-how for dynamic careers in global destination management, eco-tourism development, flight and ticketing operations, convention logistics, and international hospitality enterprises.

Program Objectives

  • Master global tour operations, ticketing systems, and large-scale event management skills.
  • Promote sustainable cultural, environmental, and ecological tourism development practices.
  • Demonstrate high-level foreign language competency, customer service excellence, and cross-cultural communication.
  • Understand travel safety protocols, risk assessment, and tourism marketing campaigns.

Curriculum Timeline

  • Year 1: Micro Perspective of Tourism, Tourism Risk Management, Philippine Culture and Tourism Geography
  • Year 2: Foreign Languages for Tourism, Travel Agency & Tour Operations, Transportation Management
  • Year 3: Destination Marketing, MICE Management, E-Commerce in Tourism
  • Year 4: Comprehensive Tourism Practicum (Local or International Internship)

Career Opportunities

  • Tour Operations Manager, Flight Attendant, Travel Consultant, Events & Convention Planner, Resort Operations Supervisor, Tourism Promotion Officer
Requirements: Form 138, Good Moral Certificate, Medical Clearance, PSA Birth Certificate

4-Year Program
BSEd

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Secondary Education (BSEd) program trains future high school educators, imparting specialized pedagogy and deep academic content knowledge across majors such as Mathematics, English, Science, and Social Studies to shape competent and inspiring secondary classroom leaders.

4-Year Program
BTVTEd

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Technical Vocational Teacher Education (BTVTEd) program equips student-teachers with advanced technical craftsmanship and pedagogical skills required to teach technical-vocational courses across senior high schools and technical training institutions.

  • Implement modern competency-based Technical, Vocational, and Education Training (TVET) standards.
  • Equip learners with hands-on practical skills aligned with global workforce demands.

  • Year 4: Industry Immersion, Technical Vocational Practice Teaching Internship

Career Opportunities

  • TVET Instructor, Senior High School TVL Teacher, Vocational Training Center Trainer, Technical Skills Assessor, Industrial Workshop Supervisor
Requirements: Form 138 / SHS Card, Good Moral Certificate, PSA Birth Certificate

College of Accountancy

Bachelor of Science in Accountancy

4-Year Program
BSA

Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Accountancy (BSA) program is a rigorous, highly specialized academic course designed to prepare students for corporate financial practice, managerial accounting, taxation compliance, and successful completion of the Certified Public Accountant (CPA) licensure examination.

Program Objectives

  • Demonstrate exceptional expertise in International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and financial statement preparation.
  • Execute detailed auditing procedures, forensic checks, and tax compliance strategies accurately.
  • Uphold the highest standards of professional ethics, integrity, and accountability.
  • Analyze complex financial statements to guide executive business decision-making.

Curriculum Timeline

  • Year 1: Financial Accounting & Reporting, Intermediate Accounting, Business Law
  • Year 2: Advanced Accounting, Cost Accounting, Income Taxation, Business Taxation
  • Year 3: Auditing Theory, Auditing Problems, Accounting Information Systems, Strategic Cost Management
  • Year 4: CPA Review Integration, Advanced Auditing, Professional Accountancy Apprenticeship

Career Opportunities

  • Certified Public Accountant (CPA), External Auditor, Internal Auditor, Tax Consultant, Chief Financial Officer, Financial Controller, Budget Analyst
Requirements: Form 138 (High Math/English Grades), Entrance Exam, Screening Interview, Good Moral

College of Computer Studies

Associate in Computer Technology

2-Year Program
ACT

Program Overview

The Associate in Computer Technology (ACT) is an intensive 2-year diploma undergraduate course focusing on practical computing, computer hardware servicing, basic software programming, network troubleshooting, and day-to-day IT systems operations for rapid entry into the tech workforce.

Program Objectives

  • Master PC hardware assembly, diagnosis, repair, and network configuration basics.
  • Develop fundamental web pages, scripts, and relational database queries.
  • Prepare graduates for immediate technical employment or lateral ladderized advancement into the BSCS degree program.
  • Execute basic IT helpdesk support and user assistance protocols.

Curriculum Timeline

  • Year 1: Fundamentals of IT, Computer Hardware Servicing, Web Design Basics, Computer Programming 1
  • Year 2: Database Management, LAN Essentials, Technical Troubleshooting, Practical IT Internship

Career Opportunities

  • IT Support Technician, Computer Maintenance Specialist, Web Developer Assistant, Network Assistant, Technical Helpdesk Agent
Requirements: Form 138 / SHS Card, Good Moral Certificate, PSA Birth Certificate
