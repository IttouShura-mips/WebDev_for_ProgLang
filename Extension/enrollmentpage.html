Statement of Undertaking

As a student of Interworld Colleges Foundation Inc. (ICF), I recognize my duties and responsibilities upon enrollment at ICF. In view of this, I declare that I understand and agree to the following:

  1. To fully subscribe to and comply with the academic, discipline, and administrative policies and requirements of ICF.
  2. To fully subscribe to and comply with the processes and schedules of Interworld Colleges Foundation, Inc.
  3. To fully acknowledge that any and all previous enrollment at my former school shall, for academic record purposes, be considered and treated as enrollment at ICF.
  4. To fully cooperate in submitting documents which are necessary to complete the requirements of Interworld Colleges Foundation Inc.
