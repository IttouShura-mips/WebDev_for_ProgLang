What Happens Next?

Here's what you can expect after successfully submitting your enrollment application.

Document Verification

Our Registrar's Office will evaluate your submitted registration details and uploaded admission requirements.

Confirmation Notice

You will receive an official confirmation via SMS or Email regarding your admission status within 1–2 business days.

Once approved, your student portal account credentials will be sent to your registered email address.
