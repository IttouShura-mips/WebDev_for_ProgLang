History & Legacy

Established in Paniqui, Tarlac, Interworld Colleges Foundation Inc. (ICF) was founded with the ambition to uplift local communities by offering relevant college and vocational degrees.

Over the years, ICF has evolved into a vibrant academic hub, constantly updating its curricula to align with industry demand, technological advancements, and national standards.

Educational Philosophy

At ICF, we believe education is a transformative partnership. We blend theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical application to prepare our students for competitive real-world careers.

Our student-centered outcomes ensure graduates leave with technical mastery, critical thinking skills, and strong moral character.
